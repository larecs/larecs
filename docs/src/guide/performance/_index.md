[Measure and tune a simulation](measure) starts with checked particle results,
then measures complete steps and profiles dominant systems. Use it after the
[core simulation](../../start/first_simulation), before choosing threading,
device execution, copy reuse, or spatial maintenance cadence.

The existing [benchmark methodology and reports](../benchmarks) retain scoped
measurements and regression-check details. They remain evidence about their
recorded workload, compiler, revision, and hardware.
